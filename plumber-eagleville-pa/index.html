Sewer backups in a township system

Many homes in the area are connected to township sewer lines, and a backup in the basement floor drain is the typical emergency. If the problem is the lateral, we clear it. If it is the public main, we tell you to call the township.

If backups keep returning, a camera inspection tells us whether roots or a broken section are responsible. Our sewer inspection page explains how that works.

Which ZIP do you have?

Addresses in Eagleville may carry 19403, the Norristown ZIP, or 19408, the Eagleville ZIP. The same plumbers serve both. If your mail says Norristown but you are in Lower Providence, you are still in our range.

Knowing your township matters for permits and for the sewer system. If you are unsure which public system you are on, check your bill or ask the township.

According to the U.S. Census Bureau, Eagleville is a census designated place in Lower Providence Township, covering about one and a half square miles. Lower Providence Township handles local permits, so ask it which apply to your project.

Winter calls in a suburban township

In winter, calls in Eagleville tend to involve frozen hose bibs, pipes in garages and crawlspaces, and water heaters that struggle in cold basements. A frozen line that has not yet burst is a warning. Open the tap, warm the pipe slowly and call if it does not thaw.

Disconnect hoses and shut off exterior spigots before the first freeze. A frost free spigot needs its hose removed to drain properly.

Public buildings and larger facilities

Eagleville includes public buildings such as the township administration building, the county correctional facility and the library. Those sites have their own facilities teams and procurement rules. We do not claim to serve any of them.

For homes nearby, a plumbing problem at a public building does not change your service. If you see a street leak or a water main break, call the township or the water utility.
