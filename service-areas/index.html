A note on each town

Bridgeport is a small borough directly across the Schuylkill River from Norristown, with older rowhouses and a bridge that dates to 1912. King of Prussia is a census designated place in Upper Merion Township, known for its mall, its offices and the nearby national historical park, and it uses ZIP code 19406.

Blue Bell sits in Whitpain Township and uses 19422, with 19424 assigned to a single organization. Collegeville is a borough on the Perkiomen Creek with its own water and sewer authority and ZIP code 19426. Eagleville is in Lower Providence Township and uses 19408, with 19415 assigned to a single organization and some addresses carrying the Norristown ZIP 19403.

How we decide where to go

We cover the towns above from one phone line. Distance matters less than what is happening. A burst pipe or a sewer backup gets a quick answer even when the address is at the edge of our range, and a scheduled job is booked when a crew can be there.

Water and sewer differ from town to town

Who supplies your water and who runs your sewer depends on where you live. In Norristown, water comes from Pennsylvania American Water and the public sewer is run by the Norristown Municipal Waste Authority, which also serves part of West Norriton. Collegeville has its own joint water and sewer authority with Trappe, drawing from wells.

That matters on a service call. If a blockage is in a public main or a leak is at the street, the utility handles it. If it is on your side of the connection, we handle it. We ask early so that you do not wait for the wrong party.

Rivers, creeks and basements

The Schuylkill River runs past Norristown and Bridgeport, with Stony Creek and Saw Mill Run joining it in Norristown. The Perkiomen Creek runs along Collegeville. Low basements near any of these can take on groundwater in heavy rain, which is why sump pump calls rise in storms and why we suggest a battery backup.

A pump cannot stop a river from overflowing, but it handles the groundwater and local rain that cause most basement water. For persistent seepage, see our page on basement waterproofing.

Winter planning for every town on this list

Southeastern Pennsylvania has real freezing weather, and burst pipes follow cold snaps in every one of these towns. Pipes along exterior walls, in garages and in crawlspaces are the usual victims. Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls, let a faucet drip during a hard freeze and disconnect outdoor hoses before the first frost.

If a pipe does burst, shut off the main water valve first and then call us. Our page on burst pipe repair lists the steps to take while we are on the way.

Older housing across the area

Norristown, Bridgeport and Collegeville are older boroughs with houses built over many decades. King of Prussia, Blue Bell and Eagleville grew mostly in the later twentieth century. The age of the house shapes the pipes inside it, so we prepare differently for each call.

Norristown ZIP codes

In Norristown itself we serve ZIP codes 19401 and 19403, the two standard ZIPs for street addresses, along with the 19404 post office box ZIP for mailing addresses in the borough. If you have a Norristown mailing address but live in a neighboring township, you are probably still within our range.

Not sure whether your address is in range? Call and give us the street. We tell you straight away.
