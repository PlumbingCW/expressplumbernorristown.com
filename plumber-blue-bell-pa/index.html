Whitpain Township housing

Whitpain is a suburban township with mostly later twentieth century homes and some older properties around the historic center. The Whitpain Public School building has been listed on the National Register of Historic Places, a reminder that parts of the area date back well before the newer subdivisions.

In the older parts, galvanized and early copper lines can show their age. In the newer parts, the usual issues are water heaters and supply lines. We tailor the repair to the house we are in.

According to the U.S. Census Bureau, Blue Bell is a census designated place within Whitpain Township. Permit questions for plumbing work go to the township, and we help gather what it asks for.

Older homes around the historic center

Parts of Blue Bell date back long before the newer subdivisions, and the pipes in those homes can show it. Galvanized supply lines lose flow as rust narrows them. Cast iron drains can crack at the bottom of the pipe.

We check what material is in the house before we recommend a repair. If pipes are worn through, repiping may be a better path than repeated patches.

Heavy rain and landscaping drainage

Subdivision lots are graded to move water away from houses, but landscaping can change that over time. A planting bed against the foundation, a patio that settles toward the house, or a downspout that was moved can send water to the basement wall.

If water shows up after rain, check the outside first. Our team can help you tell a grading problem from a plumbing leak.
