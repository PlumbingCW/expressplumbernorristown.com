Collegeville is a borough on the Perkiomen Creek, incorporated in 1896 and home to Ursinus College, which opened in 1869. Main Street and Ridge Pike carry traffic through town, and the Perkiomen Bridge dates back to 1799.

We serve Collegeville with emergency and scheduled plumbing for homes along Main Street, near the college and across the borough.

Your own water and sewer system

Collegeville is served by the Collegeville and Trappe joint public works and municipal authority, which runs its own water and sewer system. Its public water comes from wells in the Brunswick Formation, according to the authority.

The authority has published a lead service line inventory, and some lines on it are listed as lead status unknown. It has also issued notices about certain contaminants. If you want to know what your service line is made of or what is in your water, ask the authority directly. We help with plumbing on the house side and can point you to them for the rest.

According to the authority that runs Collegeville's water and sewer, the borough's public water comes from groundwater wells. If you want to know what is in your water, ask the authority for its published water quality notices.

Pre 1965 homes and copper lines

The authority has noted that copper has been required in water service and plumbing specifications in the borough since at least 1965, and it offers an inspection program for homes built before that date. If your home predates 1965, the plumbing may include materials worth a look.

We can help you read what is in the house, such as galvanized lines, old copper and fixture connections. For failing lines, repiping is the lasting answer.

Services we bring to ZIP 19426

Typical 19426 calls involve water service line repair when pressure drops, leak detection when bills climb, and drain cleaning when basement drains back up.

We also install and repair water heaters, see water heater repair. Student rental owners often call us for after hours toilet repair.

Student rentals and landlord calls

Houses near Ursinus College are often rented to students, which means high use and tenants who may not report a slow drain until it is a flood. Landlords benefit from a quick contact for after hours calls, and a tenant guide that says where the main shutoff is.

We coordinate access with the tenant and send the landlord a clear note on what we found. For a repeat clog, we recommend a camera look so the cause is known.

Creek flooding and low basements

The Perkiomen Creek runs along the borough and low spots near the water can take on groundwater in heavy rain. A sump pump with a battery backup is the standard protection.

If your basement floods when the creek is high, a pump alone may not be enough, and we tell you what a pump can and cannot do. Flooding from the creek itself is a different kind of problem.
