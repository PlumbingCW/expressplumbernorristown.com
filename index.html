Norristown plumbing

A borough with old pipes and a river nearby

Norristown became a borough in 1812 and has been the Montgomery County seat since 1784. Much of its housing is old, with downtown on a plateau and neighborhoods in the West End, East End and North End. Older houses mean older supply lines, cast iron drains and shutoff valves that no longer turn.

The borough sits on the Schuylkill River, with Stony Creek and Saw Mill Run flowing into it. Low areas have seen flooding, including during Hurricane Ida in September 2021, according to the U.S. Geological Survey. A sump pump and a working main shutoff are worth checking before the next storm.

Water comes from Pennsylvania American Water and the public sewer is run by the Norristown Municipal Waste Authority. Knowing where your side of each line ends helps you decide who to call, and we help you sort that out on the phone. See our pages on leak detection and sewer line repair for the common cases.
