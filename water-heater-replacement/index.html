When a tank rusts through, the water heater is finished. You may see a slow pool at the base, a stream from a seam, or hot water that turns brown. Replacement is the answer, and the faster it is done the less damage the water does to the room around it.

We keep this page for the urgent case. If you are planning ahead for a heater that is simply old, our water heater installation page covers the scheduled version.

The first hour after a tank fails

Close the cold water valve above the tank and turn off the power or gas. Open a hot tap somewhere upstairs to relieve pressure and help the tank drain. If water is spreading, call us while you shut things off so we can plan the right heater and the crew.

Document the damage with photographs. If water reached drywall or flooring, many people also call their insurance company. We do not handle claims, but we can describe what we found and what we replaced.

Safety items we check on every swap

A new heater needs a working temperature and pressure relief valve with a proper discharge pipe, a code appropriate shutoff and sound connections. On gas units we check the flue draws and that there is combustion air. On electric units we check the circuit matches the heater.

We also look at the expansion tank. In a house with a check valve or a pressure reducing valve on the supply, water cannot expand backward and pressure can climb. An expansion tank handles that, and an old heater may never have had one.

Signs your tank is at the end

Rust colored hot water

Corrosion inside the tank is releasing flakes.

Wet spot at the base

A weep from the shell, not a fitting, means the steel is failing.

Repeated repairs

If you are paying for parts every few months the tank is telling you something.

Tank is well past its typical life

Most tank heaters wear out. If the unit is old and showing symptoms, planning ahead beats a flood.

Replacing heaters in Norristown

Norristown homes range from older rowhouses with tight basement utility rooms to newer construction in the nearby townships. We measure the space and the route a new heater will travel before we promise a size, because a tank that does not fit down a basement stair is a slow day for everyone.

The Municipality of Norristown handles permits in the borough. Whether your replacement needs one depends on the work, so ask the Municipality directly and we will help with the information they request.

A replacement has to meet the manufacturer's installation guidelines and the plumbing and gas codes that apply locally. Model codes such as the IPC call for a relief valve discharge pipe and thermal expansion control where a check valve closes the system.

Why tank replacement costs depend on the house

Two houses can need very different jobs for the same heater. One has a clean flue and a drain a few feet away, the other needs a new vent pipe and a discharge line routed across the basement. The first takes a short visit. The second can take most of a day.
