A sewer backup is the most urgent drain problem a house can have, because the water that comes up is wastewater and it arrives at the lowest point in the building. In many Norristown homes that is a basement floor drain, a laundry tub or a shower stall.

Our job on a call like this is to stop the backup, find the cause and tell you honestly whether it was a one time blockage or a line that needs repair.

Why main sewer lines stop up

Most main line stoppages come from four things: grease and wipes building up in the pipe, tree roots working into joints, a belly or sag where solids settle, and a collapsed or offset section. A cable machine fixes the first and sometimes the second. The last two need a camera and often a repair.

The age of the pipe matters too. Houses in an older borough can still be connected with clay or cast iron lines that were laid when the street was first built up, and those materials crack, shift and let roots in more readily than modern plastic.

Before the plumber arrives

These steps protect your health and your belongings while we head to you. They are the same whether the backup is small or large.

Stop all water

No flushing, no laundry, no dishwasher, no showers until the line is clear.

Keep people away

Children and pets should stay out of any wet area. Wastewater carries bacteria.

Move what you can

Lift boxes, rugs and electronics off the floor if you can do it without stepping into standing water.

Do not use chemicals

Drain cleaners do not clear a main line and they add risk for the technician.

How we clear the main line

We open the main cleanout if there is one and run a main line machine with a cutter sized to the pipe. Roots and grease come out on the cable, and we run water afterward to see whether the line flows freely.

If the cable stops at the same spot each time or the water drains slowly even after a clean, we recommend a camera inspection. You can read about that on our sewer inspection page. A clean without a camera is a guess about what is left in the pipe.

Sewer lines and the borough sewer in Norristown

Wastewater in Norristown is collected by the Norristown Municipal Waste Authority, which also serves part of West Norriton. Your lateral from the house to the street connection is generally your responsibility, while the main in the street belongs to the authority.

That split matters on a backup call. If we clear your lateral and the water still will not move, the blockage may be in the public main, and the authority should be called at once. We help you work out which side the problem is on.

According to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, wipes, grease and other debris are common causes of sewer blockages and backups. Keeping them out of drains and toilets is the cheapest prevention there is.

If the backup keeps returning

A line that backs up twice in a year is telling you something. Either the pipe has a defect or something is being flushed that should not be. A camera inspection costs less than a third emergency visit and tells you what to fix.

If we find a broken or offset section, we cover the repair options on our sewer line repair page. If the pipe is sound and only buildup is the issue, regular cleaning or hydro jetting may be enough.
